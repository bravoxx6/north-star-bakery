Our Breads, Pastries, and Cakes

Everything on our shelves is made from scratch in our kitchen. Selection changes with the seasons, so the best way to guarantee your favorite is to send a pre-order request. We don't take payment online; you pay when you pick up.

Breads

Our breads are slow-fermented for deep flavor and a crackly crust. Baked fresh daily.

Country Sourdough

Our everyday loaf: tangy, open crumb, and a dark, blistered crust.

Seeded Multigrain

Whole wheat with flax, sunflower, and pumpkin seeds. Hearty and great for sandwiches.

Rye and Signature Loaf

A dense, flavorful rye and our famous North Star Signature Loaf. Read the Signature Loaf story.

Price range: $6 to $11 per loaf.

Pastries

Flaky, buttery, and perfect with coffee on the way to work.

Croissants

Plain butter, chocolate, and almond, laminated by hand every morning.

Scones and Muffins

Seasonal flavors like blueberry lemon, pumpkin spice, and cranberry orange.

Cookies

Chocolate chunk, iced sugar, and almond sandwich cookies, available by the piece or by the dozen.

Price range: $2.50 to $5 each. Dozens are available by pre-order.

Cakes

From small family birthdays to large community events, our cakes are made to order.

Everyday Cakes

Six-inch and eight-inch rounds in chocolate, vanilla bean, and carrot. Often available in our case on weekends.

Celebration and Event Cakes

Custom tiered and sheet cakes for weddings, graduations, and office parties. Please request at least one week ahead.

Price range: $28 to $45 for everyday cakes. Custom cakes start at $60 and are quoted per request.

How to Order

  1. Choose your items from the list above.
  2. Fill out our pre-order request form with your pickup date.
  3. We'll email you to confirm your order within one business day.
  4. Pick up and pay in the shop.
