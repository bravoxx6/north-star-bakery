Welcome to North Star Bakery

Good Morning, Neighbor

Every morning before sunrise, our ovens fill the block with the smell of fresh bread. Whether you're grabbing a croissant on your commute, picking up a loaf for family dinner, or planning a cake for a big celebration, there's a warm spot for you at our counter.

What We're Known For

  • Slow-fermented sourdough made with a starter we've fed every day for over ten years.
  • Buttery, flaky pastries laminated by hand each morning.
  • Custom celebration cakes for birthdays, weddings, and neighborhood events.
  • Local ingredients from regional farms and mills whenever possible.
