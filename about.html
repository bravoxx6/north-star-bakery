About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started in 2014 with one small oven, one sourdough starter, and a simple idea: every neighborhood deserves a place where bread is made by hand. Founder Maria Lindqvist sold her first loaves at the Saturday farmers market. When neighbors started knocking on her door on weekdays, she knew it was time to open a shop.

Today we bake for commuters, families, schools, and local event planners, but we still shape every loaf by hand, just as we did on day one.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread starts with good ingredients. We keep our list short and our partners close to home.

  • Flour: Stone-milled wheat and rye from a family mill less than 60 miles away.
  • Dairy and eggs: Butter, milk, and eggs from regional farms.
  • Fruit: Seasonal berries, apples, and pumpkins from local growers.
  • No shortcuts: No artificial preservatives, just time, care, and real ingredients.

Meet Our Team
